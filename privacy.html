Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This application ("Oddly True Pipeline") is a personal automation tool operated by a single individual to publish their own short-form videos to their own social media accounts. It has no other users, does not collect data from any third party, and does not sell, share, or otherwise distribute any data.

The only data processed is: (1) video files and captions authored by the owner, and (2) OAuth access tokens issued by TikTok, Instagram/Meta, and YouTube/Google to the owner's own developer application, used exclusively to publish the owner's own content to the owner's own accounts on those platforms.

No personal data belonging to any other individual is collected, stored, or processed by this application.

For questions, contact the repository owner via GitHub.
